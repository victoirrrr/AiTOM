# AiTOM

Agentic AI operating models, learned as a game. Eight chapters, callback quizzes, eleven real case files, flashcards and a boss level.

- `index.html` is the whole app (static, no build step). Light, dark and auto appearance. Progress is saved on the device.
- `content.js` holds everything you read: chapters, cards, questions, case files, glossary and sources. Edit it to change the content. For multiple-choice questions the first option is the correct one; the app shuffles them.
- `sw.js` and `manifest.webmanifest` make it installable and usable offline.

## Chapters

1. The Shift
2. The Autonomy Dial
3. Why Pilots Stall
4. The Operating Model
5. The Two Ledgers
6. The Use Case Atlas
7. The Tollgates
8. Boss Level: Meridian (a fictional company)

## Hosting

Served by GitHub Pages from the `main` branch root. Turn it on once under Settings, Pages, "Deploy from a branch", `main`, `/ (root)`.

Company figures come from public reports and disclosures as of late 2026; the list is in the app under Me, Sources. Frameworks such as the autonomy levels, the five layers and the tollgate thresholds are practitioner models.
